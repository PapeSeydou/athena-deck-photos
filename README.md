# ATHENA deck photos

Photo research for a private sales deck for ATHENA, a school management platform used in Senegal and West Africa.

Every photo here is a real photograph of Black African students, teachers, parents and school
environments, sourced from Unsplash and Pexels (plus a few openly licensed archives for building
exteriors). Nothing is AI-generated. Each file is at least 2400 px on its long edge and under 8 MB.

## How the repo is laid out

    photos/<SLOT>/<SLOT>_<n>.jpg    the images, one folder per deck slot
    contact-sheets/<SLOT>.jpg       thumbnails with filenames, one per slot
    contact-sheets/ALL.jpg          overview of every photo
    sources.csv                     filename, slot, source URL, photographer/site, width x height

## Slots

Filled in as the research lands.

## A note on people

Students are aged roughly 4 to 18: preschool, primary, middle school and high school. There are no
university or college students in this set.
